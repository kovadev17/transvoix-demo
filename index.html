FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How does TransVoix preserve my voice? +
TransVoix uses real-time voice cloning technology. When you speak, we analyze your voice characteristics and generate speech in the target language that sounds like you. The process takes under 2 seconds for initial cloning and runs continuously during the call.
Is my data stored? +
No. TransVoix processes audio in real-time and immediately discards it. We never store audio recordings, transcripts, or voice models. Your conversations are yours alone.
What languages do you support? +
We currently support 30+ language pairs including English, Spanish, French, Mandarin, Arabic, Hindi, German, Japanese, Korean, and more. We are continuously adding new languages based on demand.
Do I need to download an app? +
No. TransVoix works through your existing phone. Just dial through our service or add us to your current phone system. No downloads, no setup, no hardware changes.
Is TransVoix HIPAA compliant? +
TransVoix is SOC 2 Type II compliant and we are actively working toward HIPAA certification. For healthcare organizations with specific compliance needs, contact our sales team for a custom solution.
